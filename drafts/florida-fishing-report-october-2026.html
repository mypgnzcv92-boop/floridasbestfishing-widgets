Fishing Report · Statewide

October is the month Florida's regulation calendar does the most work. Gag grouper shut on the 1st, snook came open in the last two regions the same morning, Atlantic red snapper returns for a fortnight in the middle of the month, and stone crab season opens while flounder closes — on the same day. Here is every date that changes, what is biting around it, and the one October trip that needs paperwork before you leave the dock.

Every season, size and bag figure below was verified against myfwc.com on September 30, 2026. Regulations change — check before you go.

What Changed on October 1

Two things flipped at 12:01 a.m., in opposite directions:

FisheryChangeWhat it means
Gag grouper (Gulf)ClosedThe thirty-day season ran Sept 1–30 and will not reopen in 2026. The Atlantic has been shut since Aug 2. Catch-and-release statewide. Full detail →
Snook (Charlotte Harbor & Southwest)OpenThe last two closed regions came in, a month behind the rest. All nine management regions are now open at once — the only such window of 2026. Dates by region →

If you fish Boca Grande, Pine Island Sound, Matlacha, Fort Myers, Naples or Marco, this is your first legal snook since April. Slot is 28–33″ total length on the Gulf, one fish per person per day, snook permit required on top of your saltwater licence.

Worth marking now: that statewide window is not long. The Gulf closes again December 1 and the Atlantic December 15.

Two of these catch people out. Stone crab and flounder flip on the same day in opposite directions, so October 15 is simultaneously the start of one season and the end of another — see the stone crab guide and the flounder guide. And hogfish is a zone rule, not a statewide one: Gulf hogfish stays open year-round at 14″ fork length, five per harvester.

Atlantic Red Snapper, Oct 9–22: Do the Paperwork First

This is the headline trip of the month and the one most likely to go wrong before it starts. NOAA issued Florida's revised exempted fishing permit on September 21 and FWC signed the executive order the following day, which replaced the dates that had been circulating since May.

The season is one continuous block, October 9–22, in Atlantic state and federal waters from the Georgia line to the Keys. One fish per person per day, no minimum size limit, inside a ten-fish snapper-grouper aggregate of which only one may be a red snapper. For-hire captains and crew may not retain.

Before you leave the dock, every angler 16 and over must:

  • Declare the trip in FWC's Atlantic Red Snapper Reporting System. FWC is explicit that anglers who do not declare a red snapper trip may not keep red snapper.
  • Carry a copy of the exempted fishing permit on board.
  • Hold the free State Reef Fish Angler designation (private vessels) — the same designation the Gulf reef fisheries require.
  • For-hire operations must additionally join the free Atlantic For-Hire Reef Fish Registry.

⚠️ Ignore any October 2–4 or 16–18 dates you see elsewhere. Those came from the original permit application and are not in effect; a lot of third-party sites never updated. October 2–4 is closed. What's Biting

Atlantic beaches and inlets. The mullet run is on its back end, which is usually when the biggest fish show up rather than the most. Snook, tarpon and jacks stay on the bait pods through early October, and the bull redfish run takes over at the inlets as water temperatures slide. Now that every snook region is open, the mullet run and a legal slot fish finally overlap statewide.

Gulf bottom. Gag is off the table, but the structure is not. Red grouper (20″) and black grouper (24″) are open year-round in Gulf state waters, amberjack runs to the 13th, and red snapper is on its fall schedule — so a single trip to the same ledges still fills a box. Cooler water and calmer seas make October the most comfortable month of the year to run offshore.

Inshore, both coasts. Falling water temperature is the whole story. Redfish school up, seatrout move back onto the grass flats after the summer heat, and flounder stage near inlets and passes on their way offshore — worth fishing hard before the 15th, because that is the last harvest day until December. Our solunar bite times and tide charts are the quickest way to pick the window.

The Keys and the southwest. Hogfish is the clock here — the Atlantic-side and south-of-Cape-Sable season ends October 31. Stone crab traps can go in the water from October 5 but may not be tended until opening day on the 15th.

Bottom Line

October is a month to read the calendar before you load the boat. Gag is done, snook is open everywhere until December, Atlantic red snapper is a fourteen-day window that needs a declaration, and October 15 opens stone crab while it closes flounder. Get the free State Reef Fish Angler designation sorted now if you do not have it — it gates the two best offshore trips of the month.

Plan the run from the offshore hub, check conditions on the tools pages, and if you only get one trip this month, make it before the 13th — that is the last day amberjack, red snapper and both year-round groupers are all legal together.
