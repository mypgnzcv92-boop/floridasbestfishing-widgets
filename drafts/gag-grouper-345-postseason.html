Season Guide · Gulf Bottom Fishing

Gulf gag grouper closed at 12:01 a.m. on October 1, 2026 and stays closed for the rest of the year. It was a thirty-day season — September 1 to 30 — and the next Gulf opener will not come around until FWC sets the 2027 dates. The Atlantic closed back on August 2.

Here is the season as it actually ran, the limits that applied, and — more usefully if you are reading this with a boat in the driveway — the Gulf bottom fish that are still legal over the same structure.

Gag Grouper Season 2026: The Dates

Gulf state waters were open September 1–30, 2026, closing at 12:01 a.m. on October 1. FWC set those dates by executive order at its August meeting, deliberately matching NOAA's federal season so anglers weren't tracking two different calendars. Monroe County was never part of the Gulf season — the Keys follow the Atlantic rule.

The Atlantic closed first. The 2026 Atlantic gag season ran from May 1 and was ended early by executive order on August 2. Both coasts are now shut, and gag is catch-and-release statewide until the 2027 seasons are announced.

Verified against FWC's grouper page on September 30, 2026. Regulations change — check before you go.

Corrected August 16, 2026: an earlier version of this guide listed Gulf state waters as closing September 14, with federal waters running to October 1. FWC's August executive order aligned state waters with the federal season, so both ran September 1–30.

Size and Bag Limits That Applied in 2026

Worth knowing now, because these are the numbers most likely to carry into 2027:

Rule2026 Gulf
Minimum size24″ total length
Daily bag2 gag per person
AggregateCounts inside the 4-grouper aggregate — no more than 4 grouper of all species combined, per person, per day
PermitState Reef Fish Angler designation required

That last one catches people out every single opener, and it is worth sorting now rather than the night before next season. The State Reef Fish Angler designation is free, takes about two minutes, and is not the same thing as your saltwater fishing licence. Every angler harvesting reef fish from a private boat in Gulf waters needs it — and it is the same designation the October Atlantic red snapper season requires.

What's Still Open on the Same Structure

Gag closing does not mean the bottom season is over. The ledges and wrecks you would have fished in September are holding fish you can still put in the box:

All four verified against FWC on September 30, 2026.

Red grouper is the obvious swap. Same rods, same rigs, same bottom machine — and with a four-inch-smaller minimum it is a far more forgiving fish to put a limit together on. Work hard bottom from about 40 feet out past 120, and fish a little lighter than you would for gag; red grouper do not run for the hole with the same violence.

If you only get one more offshore day this year, make it before October 13 — that is the last day amberjack and red snapper and both year-round groupers are all legal on the same trip.

Where Gag Live — and How to Be Ready for 2027

Gag are still out there and still eat; you just have to let them go. If you are scouting for next September, early-season gag sit shallower than most people expect. Start on nearshore ledges, hard bottom and artificial reefs in 40–90 feet, then work deeper structure past 100 feet for the better fish.

What you're looking for is ambush structure with current pushing bait across it — live bottom, rock piles, wrecks, reef edges. Idle and read your bottom machine before you drop. One productive ledge will out-produce a day of hopping between marks, and marks you find now will still be holding fish next fall.

Releasing a gag from depth means barotrauma. FWC requires a descending device or venting tool, rigged and ready, on board any vessel fishing for reef fish in state waters, and requires you to use one if a fish shows signs of barotrauma. A descending device is the more forgiving of the two if you have not practised venting.

Tackle and Technique

Gag eat and immediately bolt for the hole. The entire game is stopping them in the first three seconds, which is a tackle problem before it's a skill problem — and the same setup covers red grouper and amberjack right now:

  • Heavy-power rod under 7 feet, and a sealed high-capacity reel with the drag locked down.
  • 50–80 lb braid to a heavy fluorocarbon leader — you need to turn the fish's head immediately.
  • A knocker or fish-finder rig with enough lead to hold bottom, and a non-offset circle hook in 5/0–8/0.
  • Baits: live pinfish and grunts, big threadfins or sardines, or cut squid and bonito strips. Live bait draws the bigger fish.

Bottom Line

Gulf gag closed at 12:01 a.m. October 1, 2026 in both state and federal waters and will not reopen this year; the Atlantic has been shut since August 2. Twenty-four inches and two per person inside the four-grouper aggregate were the 2026 rules, and the free State Reef Fish Angler designation is still what you need for every other reef-fish trip you take this fall.

Meanwhile the structure is still worth running to: red and black grouper are open year-round, amberjack runs to October 13, and red snapper is on its fall schedule into January. Gag is simply the one you photograph and send back.
